Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Pocket Vending Machine does not ask for your name, does not need an account and shows no ads. This policy explains what little information the game uses and why.

Pocket Vending Machine ("the game") is made by Onnoel Games ("we", "us"). It applies to the game on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Information the game stores

Your game progress stays on your device. It includes your money, upgrades, unlocked products, purchased boosts and skins, settings and the time you last played. We do not receive or see this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

We do not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or contacts
  • your precise location
  • photos, files or other content on your device
  • advertising identifiers (the game shows no ads and does not track you across apps)

If you email us for feedback, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

iCloud backup

On iPhone and iPad, the game stores a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account (Apple's key-value storage), so you can continue on a new device. This copy is linked to your Apple ID and managed by Apple; we cannot access it. On Android, Google's standard app backup may store the same data in your Google account.

Time check

To prevent cheating by changing the device clock, the game asks a well-known website (apple.com, google.com or cloudflare.com) for the current time when it starts. Only a standard web request is made; no game data or personal information is sent. Like any website visit, that site receives your IP address.

In-app purchases

Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing, a service from Unity Technologies, to process purchases. When you make or restore a purchase, Unity receives:

  • purchase details, such as the product, price and transaction ID
  • app and device identifiers specific to this installation (no advertising IDs)
  • technical details, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • your approximate country, derived from your IP address

Unity uses this data to make purchases work, to prevent the same purchase from being counted twice, and for sales reporting. See Unity's privacy policy for details.

Notifications

The game can send reminders, for example when your vending machine is almost empty. These are scheduled on your device itself; no data is sent to a server for them. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your device settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases are protected by the parental controls of the App Store and Google Play.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone, apart from the purchase data that Unity, Apple and Google need to process purchases.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you can ask to access or delete your personal data. Because we hold no game data about you, you can delete everything by uninstalling the game. For purchase data held by Unity, contact us and we will pass your request on.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
