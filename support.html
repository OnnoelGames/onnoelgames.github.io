Frequently asked questions

I bought a boost or skin, but it's gone.
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?
Yes. Your progress is saved in your own iCloud account. Sign in with the same Apple ID on your new device and open the game.

My machine doesn't earn while I'm away.
Your machine only sells what's in it. When the slots are empty, sales stop. Fill your slots before you leave, or buy extra slot capacity.

I don't get any notifications.
Check that notifications are turned on in the game's Settings and in your iPhone settings under Notifications → Pocket Vending Machine.

I want a refund.
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
